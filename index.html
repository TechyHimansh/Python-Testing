About the platform

A live auction for the #1 spot.

RankYourInsta.io turns visibility into a real-time, real-money competition — no wallets, no chance, just whoever's willing to pay the most, right now.

The core idea

Every user lists their Instagram profile. To rank, you place a real payment — not a wallet top-up or virtual currency. The leaderboard is always sorted strictly by the highest current paid bid, highest first, and it can never be "locked in": anyone can outbid you at any moment by paying more.

A quick example

Varun bids ₹2Ranked #1
→
Ravi bids ₹4Ranked #1, Varun #2
→
Gian bids ₹5Ranked #1, Ravi #2, Varun #3
→
Varun bids ₹10Back to #1

This repeats indefinitely — there's no time limit or reserve price. It's a continuously open auction, and the board re-sorts and animates live for everyone watching.

Why people use it

Instagram's own algorithm decides who gets seen. RankYourInsta.io flips that — instead of guessing what the algorithm rewards, you're placed on a public board purely by how much you're willing to put behind your own visibility. Creators use it to drive traffic during a launch, small brands use it to get eyes on a campaign page, and some people just enjoy the competition of holding #1.

What this is — and isn't

This is a paid visibility placement, similar in spirit to a real-time advertising auction. It is not a lottery, a game of chance, or an investment product — outcomes are determined entirely by who pays the most, not by chance, and a higher bid never guarantees followers, engagement, or any outcome on Instagram itself. Full terms are covered in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data on RankYourInsta.io.

1. What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, Instagram handle, and an optional profile photo. When you place a bid, we record the amount, currency, payment method, and status (pending, paid, or failed) — never your raw card, UPI, or bank details. When you contact support or use the contact form, we keep the message you send us so we can respond.

2. How payments are handled

We never store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers. All payments are processed by a licensed third-party payment gateway (Cashfree for UPI/India, with Stripe or PayPal for international cards and PayPal). We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, its amount, and a transaction reference — the gateway handles the sensitive parts under its own PCI-compliant systems.

3. How your data is used

  • Your name, photo, Instagram handle, and current bid are shown publicly on the leaderboard — that's the core of the product, so don't upload anything you don't want visible to every visitor.
  • Your email is used only for account access, payment confirmations, and replying to support requests. We don't sell or share it with advertisers.
  • Basic, anonymized usage events (signups, bids placed, payments completed) are logged to help us understand how the platform is used and catch problems early.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use minimal session-level storage to keep you logged in during a visit and to avoid showing the welcome popup repeatedly in the same session. We don't use third-party tracking or advertising cookies.

5. Data retention

Account and bid history are retained for as long as your account is active, so your bid history and leaderboard rank stay accurate. You can request deletion at any time from Edit Profile or by contacting support — we'll remove your public listing and personal data, though we may keep anonymized transaction records as required for financial recordkeeping.

6. Your rights

  • Edit your profile photo, name, Instagram handle, or email at any time from Edit Profile.
  • Request a copy of the data we hold on you by emailing Contact@spyrnt.com.
  • Request account and data deletion at any time, subject to the retention note above.

7. Age requirement

RankYourInsta.io involves real-money payments and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to Contact@spyrnt.com or via WhatsApp at +91 76785 96887, available 24×7.
